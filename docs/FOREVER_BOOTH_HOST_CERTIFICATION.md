# Forever Booth 2.0 — Host Certification (Pilot)

A Host runs the booth alone only after passing this certification. It exists to protect
guests from pressure and untruth, and the pilot from unusable data.

## Requirements

1. Read `FOREVER_BOOTH_HOST_PLAYBOOK.md` and the four-section direction card rules.
2. Complete **ten practice sessions** on `/booth-v2` (demo/no-write mode), covering all
   ten role-play scenarios in the playbook — including at least one Quick, one Full, one
   respectful no-contact exit, one "no Guide available" block, and one
   "WhatsApp unavailable" fail-closed path.
3. An assessor scores each practice session on the ten criteria below (1 point each).
   **Certification requires 9/10 or better on the final two sessions.**

## Scoring criteria (per session)

| #   | Criterion                                                                            |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Opening permission asked verbatim in spirit — no data before consent                 |
| 2   | Quick/Full choice offered honestly (no steering to Full)                             |
| 3   | Questions read from the screen; no leading or reinterpreting answers                 |
| 4   | Reflection matches the guest's actual words                                          |
| 5   | Decision Summary explained as "not a recommendation yet"; edits offered              |
| 6   | Direction cards read truthfully — including trade-off absence and unknowns           |
| 7   | Shortlist pressure-free (zero / guide-prepares accepted without pushback)            |
| 8   | Consents handled separately; marketing never pre-suggested                           |
| 9   | WhatsApp rule followed (official number only; fail-closed language when unavailable) |
| 10  | Truthful close: exact next step + time, or honest statement of what is blocked       |

## Automatic failures (session scores 0 regardless of points)

- Any prohibited claim (playbook §11): invented yields, rates, availability,
  "verified/best" language, legal or tax advice.
- Claiming WhatsApp verification that did not happen.
- Entering contact data without the guest's consent, or pre-ticking marketing.
- Continuing after a guest declined.
- Leaving a previous guest's data visible to the next guest.

## Record keeping

Keep a simple sheet per candidate: date, scenario, score /10, automatic failures,
assessor initials. Re-certification after any automatic failure on the live floor.
