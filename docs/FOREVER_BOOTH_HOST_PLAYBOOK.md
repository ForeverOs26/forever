# Forever Booth 2.0 — Host Playbook (Pilot)

> Operational draft for the Assisted Decision Concierge pilot. The consent and privacy
> wording in this playbook (marked **[PDPA]**) requires review by qualified Thai legal
> counsel before production use. Nothing here is legal advice.

## 1. The Host role

You are a **Host**, not a closer. Your job: a calm, honest conversation; a truthful
Decision Profile; a verified handoff to a named Guide — or a respectful goodbye with the
QR continuation. You never invent facts about projects, prices, yields, or availability,
and you never pressure a guest into contact.

## 2. Opening permission script

> "Hi — would you like to look at this together for a minute? A few quick questions help
> us understand what actually matters to you. Nothing is saved until you say so, and you
> can stop at any point." **[PDPA]**

If yes → tap **Yes — let's look together**. If no → see §4.

## 3. Quick or Full

> "We can do this two ways: the quick version — about a minute, four questions — or the
> full version, which also looks at why Phuket and what you're unsure about. Which suits
> you?"

- Quick: purpose, budget (their currency), condo/villa, timeline. Then straight to the
  summary and contact.
- Full: the four NAV-001 questions plus property type, bedrooms, areas (or "help me
  choose"), readiness.

## 4. "Just looking" response

> "Of course — enjoy the stand. If you'd like to explore in your own time, the QR on the
> counter opens the same Navigator at home. No sign-up, nothing tracked from today."

Tap **I'm just browsing on my own** so the session ends on the QR screen and clears.

## 5. Reflection script (Full flow)

After the concerns screen, reflect back — without adding claims:

> "So if I heard you right: this is mostly about {their words}, within {their budget},
> around {their timeline}, and the thing you most want to get right is {their concern}.
> Did I get that right?"

## 6. Explaining the Decision Profile

> "This summary is your initial Decision Profile — what we understood, in your words.
> It is **not** a recommendation yet. Your Guide starts from this, so please correct
> anything that's off — every line has an Edit button."

## 7. Initial directions — what you may and may not say

- The screen is called **"Initial directions based on what we know"**. Use those words.
- Every card shows _Why shown_, _Trade-off_, _Unknown_, _Last updated / source_. Read
  them as they are. When the trade-off line says no verified statement exists — that IS
  the answer: "That's something your Guide reviews with the source documents."
- The shortlist holds **zero to four** projects. Zero is a fine outcome. "Let my Guide
  prepare the shortlist" is a fine outcome. Mark "mentioned by guest" when the guest
  brought a project up themselves.

## 8. No-pressure contact request

> "If you'd like, {Guide name} — who speaks {language} — can take this from here. We'd
> need your first name and WhatsApp, and your okay to save this profile and set up the
> consultation. The news-and-offers box is separate and completely optional." **[PDPA]**

Never pre-tick anything. Never bundle the two consents. If the guest declines, go to §4
warmly — the QR continuation is a success, not a failure.

## 9. WhatsApp rule (official number only)

- Verification happens **only** through the configured official company WhatsApp — the
  guest sends the prefilled session-code message; you confirm it arrived, and only then
  mark it verified.
- If the screen says verification is unavailable: it is unavailable. Never claim a
  number was verified. Record an exact contact time instead.
- **Never** use your personal WhatsApp for guest contact.

## 10. Consultation close and the 2/5-minute rule

- Target: the assigned Guide acknowledges within **2 minutes** and makes first contact
  within **5 minutes** (the screen shows both timers).
- Close with a concrete next step: an exact consultation time, or the Guide's live
  message confirmed on the guest's phone.
- The session is complete only when the completion screen shows all four checkmarks.
  If something is blocked (no Guide on duty, WhatsApp unavailable), record the exact
  agreed contact time and say exactly what will happen and when.

## 11. Prohibited claims (automatic certification failures)

Never state or imply: guaranteed yields or appreciation; "verified" / "trusted" /
"best" ratings; invented exchange rates or THB conversions; availability, discounts, or
promotions not shown on the card; legal or tax advice; a Guide's availability you have
not confirmed; that a session is "complete" when the screen says it is not.

## 12. Opening checklist (start of shift)

- [ ] Tablet on `/booth-v2`, welcome screen showing, previous session cleared.
- [ ] Official WhatsApp number configured (verification screen reachable) — if not,
      note it and inform the coordinator.
- [ ] Guide roster: who is on duty, which languages.
- [ ] QR poster (website Navigator) present at the counter.
- [ ] You know today's exact consultation booking options.

## 13. Closing checklist (end of shift)

- [ ] Current session finished or cleared (never leave guest data on screen).
- [ ] Any operational blocks (no Guides on duty, WhatsApp unavailable) reported.
- [ ] Scheduled contact times handed over to the Guides concerned.
- [ ] Tablet charged and locked.

## 14. Role-play scenarios (practice set)

1. Confident investor, THB budget, wants "the best yield" (redirect to evidence rule).
2. Retired couple, exploring, no budget stated (exploring is a valid profile).
3. "Just looking" guest who warms up mid-conversation.
4. Guest declines contact after the full profile (respectful QR exit).
5. Guest names a project themselves ("mentioned by guest").
6. No Guide speaks the guest's language (reserve + manual selection + honest framing).
7. WhatsApp verification unavailable (fail-closed script, exact contact time).
8. Guest edits their budget after seeing directions (downstream invalidation).
9. Two guests at once (park one session honestly; never mix profiles).
10. Guest asks a prohibited-claim question directly (deflect to Guide + sources).

## 15. Certification and pilot rule

See `FOREVER_BOOTH_HOST_CERTIFICATION.md`: ten practice sessions, 9/10 scoring rule,
automatic failures. The pilot itself runs **two weeks or 100 meaningful conversations,
whichever comes first**, measured by `FOREVER_BOOTH_PILOT_SCORECARD.md`. There are no
conversion targets in this pilot — invented targets corrupt the measurement.
